The mystery

Who killed Dr. Boddy?

Halloween night, 11:47 PM. Dr. Boddy, keeper of the department's microscope booking calendar, is found dead. Seven lab members were in the building. One of them is lying.

Suspects, in walk order

#SUSPECTWEAPONTHEIR ROOM

The order they walk out is part of the puzzle.

The rule

The innocent tell the truth. The killer lies. Remember the order they walked out.

The clues, one at a time

    Cast briefing

    You're a suspect. Walk out in order.

    You're on stage for about 5 minutes. The audience solves the mystery from the order you walk out, so getting the order right is the most important job. Bring your costume and a weapon big enough to see from the back row.

    Walk order

    #YOU AREHOLDINGAFTERBEFORE

    Line up backstage in this order 5 minutes before we start.

    Getting on stage

    1. Wait for the MC to call your name. That's your cue. Your photo also comes up on the screen.
    2. Walk to the center mark, face the audience, hold up your weapon and pose for about 3 seconds.
    3. Walk to your spot in the line at the back of the stage. It fills from the audience's left to right: #1 at the far left, each next person to the right of the one before.
    4. Stay in line and in character. Don't stand in front of the projector screen.

    During the clues

    The MC reads 5 clues. If one is yours (Mustard, Peach, Peacock, Plum or Scarlett), take one step forward while it's read, look very trustworthy, then step back. Mrs. White and Ms. Green have no clue: just look suspicious.

    Getting off stage

    1. When the MC says "Now it's your turn, detectives" (voting opens), walk off the way you came in, in the same order (#1 first).
    2. Stay close by. After the reveal, the MC calls everyone back on for a bow. Come back in the same order.

    Rules

    • Don't change the order, and don't swap places with anyone.
    • Don't hint at the answer, even if you've figured it out.
    • Have fun. Overact.
