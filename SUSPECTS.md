# A Lab Murder: suspect briefing 🎃

Thanks for playing a suspect! The whole bit takes about 5 minutes on stage. The audience
solves the mystery from **the order you walk out**, so getting the order right is the
most important job.

## Bring

- Your Clue costume
- Your "murder weapon" (below), big enough to see from the back of the room

## The walk order

| # | You are | Holding | Walk out after | Walk out before |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Colonel Mustard | Ice scraper ❄️ | (you go first) | Miss Scarlett |
| 2 | Miss Scarlett | Microscope 🔬 | Colonel Mustard | Miss Peach |
| 3 | Miss Peach | Textbook 📕 | Miss Scarlett | Professor Plum |
| 4 | Professor Plum | Tape gun 📦 | Miss Peach | Mrs. Peacock |
| 5 | Mrs. Peacock | Poison ☠️ | Professor Plum | Mrs. White |
| 6 | Mrs. White | Rubber hose 🪢 | Mrs. Peacock | Ms. Green |
| 7 | Ms. Green | Micropipette 💧 | Mrs. White | (you go last) |

Line up backstage in this order **5 minutes before we start**. Check who's in front of
you and who's behind you.

## Getting on stage

1. Wait for the MC to call **your name**. That's your cue (the screen shows your photo too).
2. Walk to the **center mark**, face the audience, hold up your weapon and strike a pose for about 3 seconds.
3. Walk to your spot in the **lineup at the back of the stage**. The line fills from the
   audience's left to right: #1 at the far left, each next person to the right of the one before.
4. Stay in the line, in character. Don't block the projector screen.

## During the clues

The MC reads 5 clues. If one is yours (Mustard, Peach, Peacock, Plum, Scarlett), **take
one step forward** while it's read, look very trustworthy, then step back.
Mrs. White and Ms. Green have no clue: just look suspicious.

## Getting off stage

When the MC says **"Now it's your turn, detectives"** (voting opens), walk off the way
you came in, **in the same order** (#1 first). Stay close by: after the reveal the MC
calls everyone back on for **a bow**. Come back in the same order.

## Rules

- **Don't change the order**, and don't swap places with anyone.
- Don't hint at the answer, even if you've figured it out. The innocent never look guilty… and neither does the killer.
- Have fun. Overact.
