# MC script: A Lab Murder (about 2 minutes)

Read the plain text aloud. **[Bracketed bold lines]** are cues for whoever runs the
control page. Pause where you see "…". Speak slowly and keep it low and spooky.

---

**[Control: TITLE]**

Good evening… and welcome to the darkest night of the academic year.

Halloween. Eleven forty-seven PM. The building is empty… almost.
Dr. Boddy, keeper of the department's microscope booking calendar, has been found dead.

Seven lab members were in the building tonight. One of them… is lying.
Let's meet them.

**[Control: LINEUP. Each name is the cast's cue to walk. Click Next after each pose.]**

First… **Colonel Mustard**, with the ice scraper, from the Cold Room.

**Miss Scarlett**, with the microscope, from the Elevator.

**Miss Peach**, with the textbook, from the Reading Rooms.

**Professor Plum**, with the tape gun, from Sheldon.

**Mrs. Peacock**, with the poison, from the Daily Grind.

**Mrs. White**, with the rubber hose, from the Glass Conference Room.

And last… **Ms. Green**, with the micropipette, from the Flow Core.

**[Control: RULE]**

One rule. The innocent tell the truth. The killer lies.
And remember the order they walked out… it matters.

**[Control: CLUES. The speaker steps forward. Click Next after each.]**

Colonel Mustard says: *"The body was still warm. Whatever happened, it wasn't in my Cold Room."*

Miss Peach says: *"The weapon belongs to someone who walked out right next to me."*

Mrs. Peacock says: *"It happened on the weapon owner's own turf, in their room."*

Professor Plum says: *"I was in Sheldon all night. Nothing happened there."*

And Miss Scarlett says: *"The killer walked out before I did."*

**[Control: VOTING. This opens voting and starts the 2-minute countdown. Cast exits.]**

Now it's your turn, detectives. Scan the code on the screen.
Pick one suspect, one weapon, one location. You can change your mind until the clock runs out.
When it hits zero, we'll see who you accused… and then we'll reveal who really killed Dr. Boddy.

---

*After the countdown (not part of the 2 minutes):*

**[Control: RESULTS]** "The votes are in. Here's who you accused…" Read out the top bar.

**[Control: REVEAL]** "The murderer was…" Stay quiet while the three cards flip.
The reveal line appears by itself. Then: "Let's bring our suspects back for a bow!"
